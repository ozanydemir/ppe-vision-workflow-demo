# Privacy and Security Policy

Do not add proprietary, user-supplied, or workplace-derived images, videos, labels, model weights,
employee or identity data, camera names, site names, access events, uniforms, badges, or workplace
geometry. A licensed stock photograph may appear only when credited in `ASSET_CREDITS.md`; it must
never be sent to the demo, treated as a safety event, or associated with an identity or location.

The only exception is `docs/reference/deidentified-ppe-reference-frame.png`: an owner-authorized,
low-resolution visual reference with no visible face, identity, camera, site, or production metadata.
It must remain a static README asset and may not become model input, a dataset item, a benchmark, or
an operational safety record.

The public demo intentionally avoids face recognition and re-identification. Any real deployment
would require privacy impact assessment, data retention rules, role-based access, human review, and
site-specific model validation.
