# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred: computer-vision engineers and safety teams evaluating a synthetic PPE review workflow.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate detection-event validation, PPE compliance aggregation, confidence review, and dataset
quality checks without publishing any real workplace image or identity data.

## Positioning

The repository focuses on the review and evidence system around a vision model, not an unsupported
accuracy claim or a private dataset.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Synthetic geometry and detections only.
- No faces, identities, employees, cameras, sites, datasets, media, or model weights.
- No claimed production accuracy.
- Active-development status remains visible.

## Product Principles

- Validate labels before trusting metrics.
- Treat confidence thresholds as review policy.
- Separate compliance events from identity.
- Publish no workplace imagery.
